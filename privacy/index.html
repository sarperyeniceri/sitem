Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you contact us, why we use it and what your rights are under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

Veri Creative Lab is a trading name of Equation London Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16062416).
Registered address: 22 Upper Brook Street, London, England, W1K 7PZ
Email: fthdzyl@gmail.com

We are the data controller for the personal data described below.

What we collect

  • Contact form on this website: your name, email address, your trade and the area you work in.
  • Lead forms on Facebook and Instagram: the details you choose to submit in the form, such as your name, email address, phone number, trade and area.
  • Messages: what you send us through Messenger, Instagram Direct or WhatsApp, together with your profile name and, for WhatsApp, your phone number.
  • Website usage: pages visited, clicks, device and browser type and an approximate location, collected by the measurement tools listed below.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you, answer your questions and send you a quote. Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.
  • To understand how our website and adverts perform so we can improve them. Legal basis: our legitimate interests.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for automated decisions about you.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers our website form to our email inbox.
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp), for lead forms and messages.
  • Microsoft Clarity, which records how visitors use the site (clicks, scrolling, heatmaps); text typed into forms is masked.
  • OpenAI measurement pixel, which tells us whether visitors who saw our ChatGPT adverts went on to contact us.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses.

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, the United States. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer for international transfers, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Cookies and measurement

Microsoft Clarity and the OpenAI pixel use cookies or similar technologies on this website to measure visits and adverts. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for 12 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client, in which case we keep the records we need for the contract and for tax purposes for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it or delete it;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, email us at fthdzyl@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes
