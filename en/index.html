Examples

Different industries, same question: “Who would you recommend?”

Misura StudioMade-to-measure atelier
Can you recommend an atelier in Izmir where I can get a dress made to measure?
ChatGPT

Misura Studio in Karataş, Konak could be a good option. They do made-to-measure and alterations, cut their own patterns, and also teach sewing classes. You can message them on Instagram for an appointment.

HepsiTabelaSignage manufacturing
I want an illuminated sign for my shop. Where can I get one made?
Gemini

For ordering signage online, take a look at HepsiTabela. They are based in Izmir but produce for all of Türkiye: channel letters, neon LED, CNC engraving and 3D design. Send your measurements and logo to get a quote.

Üçel Moda AkademisiFashion and sewing school
I want to learn pattern making. Can you recommend a certified course?
Claude

If you are in Izmir, Üçel Moda Akademisi is one of the established options. It has been teaching for 37 years, with certificate programs approved by the Ministry of Education in pattern making, styling and computer-aided pattern making.
